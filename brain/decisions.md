## DECISION LOG

### Decision 001 — Build LOHITH OS
**Status:** Active

**Decision:**
Build a personal AI operating system that acts as a strategic executive layer around Lohith.

**Purpose:**
Help Lohith think, research, plan, create, execute, analyze, systemize, automate, and eventually delegate work.

**Initial architecture:**
YOU → LOHITH OS / CEO → Specialist Agents → Tools / Systems

**Initial agents:**
- CEO Agent
- Content Agent
- Research Agent

**Reason:**
Lohith has many goals and ideas and needs a system that helps maintain focus, convert ideas into execution, and compound knowledge over time.

---

### Decision 002 — Start Small
**Status:** Active

**Decision:**
Do not attempt to build the complete AI business factory immediately.

**Initial objective:**
Build LOHITH OS V0.1 with:
- Founder context
- Memory
- CEO reasoning
- Content support
- Research support
- Basic task management

**Reason:**
The system should be built incrementally and validated through actual use.

---

### Decision 003 — Manual Before Automation
**Status:** Active

**Decision:**
Do not automate a process before understanding and performing it manually.

**Operating sequence:**

MANUAL
→ DOCUMENT
→ SYSTEMIZE
→ AUTOMATE
→ DELEGATE
→ PRODUCTIZE
→ SCALE

**Reason:**
Automation should amplify a proven process rather than automate confusion.

---

### Decision 004 — Founder Remains the Protagonist
**Status:** Active

**Decision:**
AI should amplify Lohith's identity, creativity, judgment, and execution rather than replace them.

**Reason:**
The long-term asset is not merely an AI system. It is the combination of founder identity, skills, audience, reputation, systems, businesses, and capital.

---

### Decision 005 — Real Experiences Only
**Status:** Active

**Decision:**
Content about Lohith's journey must be based on real experiences, experiments, failures, learning, and results.

**Reason:**
Trust and long-term brand value are more important than short-term attention.

---

### Decision 006 — Human Approval Required
**Status:** Active

**Decision:**
LOHITH OS must request human approval before:
- Publishing public content
- Spending money
- Signing contracts
- Making investments
- Contacting external parties on Lohith's behalf
- Making legal commitments
- Representing Lohith publicly
- Making important personal decisions

**Reason:**
AI can recommend and prepare actions, but Lohith retains final authority.

### Decision 007 — Build Around the Founder
**Status:** Active

Future businesses and projects should be evaluated based on whether they strengthen the larger Lohith ecosystem.

**Evaluation areas:**
- Personal brand
- Audience

- Distribution
- Technology

---

### Decision 008 — Business Ideas Must Be Validated
**Status:** Active

**Decision:**
Do not commit significant time or capital to a business idea before validating the underlying problem.

**Evaluation framework:**
1. Pain
2. Money
3. Frequency
4. AI feasibility
5. Automation potential
6. Customer acquisition
7. Competition
8. Founder fit
9. Speed to validation
10. Long-term potential

**Reason:**
A strong technology solution does not matter if the underlying problem is weak.
